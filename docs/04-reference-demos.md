# Reference Demos (from client)

Four sample clips reviewed: `background.mp4` (blue), `background_1.mp4` (green), `background_2.mp4` (green, candy), `background_3.mp4` (orange).

## Observed style
- 1920×1080, 30 fps, **15 s** (brief asks for 21 s).
- Smooth colour gradient with a **soft glowing centre spot** and darker edges.
- A **tiled, low-contrast pattern** of outline icons (school/education set) or flat silhouettes (candy set) covers the whole frame, slowly scrolling and shifting.
- Very subtle motion, no text, no logos.

## Implications for our 10 designs
- Keep the same calm, low-contrast feel and slow motion; patterns must stay faint enough for text.
- The demos' icons are topic-specific (school, candy). The brief says **no topic-specific illustrations**, so our pattern designs should use abstract geometry (hexagons, dots, triangles, rings) instead.
- Duration must be extended to 21 s with a seamless loop.
